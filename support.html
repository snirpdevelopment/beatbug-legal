Beatbug support

Beatbug is a pixel-art flyer with a music-maker built in, made by Snirp Development in Denmark.

Contact

Email snirpdevelopment@gmail.com. Please say which iPhone and which iOS version you use, and what happened.

Restoring your purchase

If you bought "Open everything" and it is not shown on a new or reset device, open Beatbug, go to Settings and tap Restore Purchases. You need to be signed in to the same Apple Account that made the purchase.

Refunds and purchase problems

Purchases are handled by Apple, so refunds and billing questions go through Apple’s App Store support, not through us.

Your data

Beatbug collects nothing. Your progress and saved loops stay on your device. See the privacy policy.
