Beatbug privacy policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026 · applies to Beatbug for iPhone, version 1.0

In short: Beatbug collects nothing about you. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it sends no information about you or your device to us or to anyone else.

Who we are

Beatbug is made by Snirp Development, a sole proprietorship (enkeltmandsvirksomhed) in Denmark, CVR 46817680. You can reach us at snirpdevelopment@gmail.com.

What we collect

Nothing. The app does not collect, receive or store any personal data on our side, and we do not know who plays it. Beatbug contains no analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting code, and no software from other companies.

The app does not ask for access to your camera, microphone, contacts, photos or location.

What stays on your device

Your progress, the pollen you have collected, the skins and worlds you have opened, your sound settings and the loops you save in the Studio are stored only on your device, in the app's own storage. They are never sent to us. Deleting the app deletes them. Backups of your device (for example iCloud Backup) are controlled by you and by Apple, not by us.

Purchases

Beatbug has one optional one-time purchase, "Open everything". The purchase is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not see or store your payment details, your name or your Apple Account. To show the price and to restore a purchase, the app uses Apple's StoreKit, which talks to the App Store on your device; we receive nothing from that exchange. Apple's own privacy policy applies to the purchase.

Network use

Beatbug itself makes no network connections to us or to any other party. The only exception is the Apple-run purchase service described above.

Children

Beatbug is made for a general audience of teenagers and adults and is not directed at children. Because we collect no personal data from anyone, we do not knowingly collect personal data from children either.

If you contact us

If you send us an email, we use your email address and your message only to reply to you, and we delete the conversation when it is no longer needed. We do not share it with anyone.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you through the app, there is nothing for us to give you a copy of, correct or delete. If you believe we hold something about you anyway, write to us and we will answer. You may also complain to your national data protection authority; in Denmark that is Datatilsynet.

Changes

If a future version of Beatbug collects any data, we will update this policy before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.
